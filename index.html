What we do

Three ways to show up

Whether you've shipped apps for years or have never written a line of code, there's always something to learn.

Once a month

Club meetings

Our regular get-together to learn, build, and connect. Expect a mix of technical and non-technical topics, plus members demoing their own projects.

Twice a semester

Workshops

Hands-on sessions where you build real skills with Claude. Topics include Claude Code and Skills, building with agents, building with MCP, and AI research discussions.

End of school year

Hackathon

Our campus-wide capstone. Team up, build something from scratch with Claude, and present it to judges. Great for your portfolio and for meeting people across majors.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most members start by describing what they want in plain English. You'll pick up how the code works along the way if you're curious.

Is it only for computer science majors?

Not at all. We have builders from business, design, journalism, the sciences, and more. Different backgrounds lead to better project ideas.

What should I bring?

A charged laptop and an idea, even a rough one. If you don't have an idea yet, we'll help you find one.

Does it cost anything?

No. Membership is free, and members can earn Claude API credits just by taking part.

How do I get the Claude credits?

Join the club and complete the check-in form at each meeting to stay eligible. You'll need a Claude Platform account (platform.claude.com) that uses your ASU email. We'll walk you through setup at a meeting.

What does "responsible AI use" mean here?

We build ambitious things and also talk openly about where AI falls short, how to check its work, and how it affects people. Using Claude well means knowing how, when, and why to use it.
